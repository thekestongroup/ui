`StatTile` can be a link: give it an `href` (or `as`, e.g. `RouterLink`, with its `to`) and the whole tile is clickable. An arrow in the corner shows it, the border darkens on hover and the background on press, and keyboard focus gets the usual ring. Static tiles are unchanged. (A link tile doesn't show the `footer` slot.)
