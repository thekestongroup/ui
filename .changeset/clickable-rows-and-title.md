Table rows can be clickable: `rowLink` gives each row a page to go to (with `linkComponent`, e.g. RouterLink). The row-header cell becomes a real link stretched over the row, so the row highlights on hover and a click anywhere on it opens the page, while buttons and links in other cells still work on their own. Header and AppShell get `titleTo`, which makes the app's name a link (e.g. to its dashboard), with hover and focus states.
